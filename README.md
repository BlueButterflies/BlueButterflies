## More than a marketplace. More than business software.

Most service workflows are fragmented across phone calls, calendars, messages, payment tools, and paperwork. VoxaFix is being built to connect the entire job lifecycle in one platform: service discovery, AI-assisted analysis, quotes, booking, dispatch, approvals, payments, invoices, and records.

**One shared job, with role-specific tools:**

| Customers | Independent Professionals | Service Companies & Technicians |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find professionals, compare quotes, book and pay | Manage quotes, jobs, calendars, earnings, and records | Coordinate employees, dispatch, availability, fleet activity, and company operations |
